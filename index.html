02 / Research

Research Themes

Exploring the physics of complex, driven systems through simulation and analytical methods.

⚛

Active Matter Physics

Self-propelled particle models (ABP, AOUP) for collective motion, motility-induced phase separation, and flocking transitions in biological and synthetic systems.

⟳

Non-Equilibrium Systems

Entropy production, fluctuation theorems, and driven-dissipative dynamics in systems far from thermal equilibrium.

◈

Complex Networks

Graph-theoretic approaches to biological interaction networks, percolation, and topological phase transitions.

◎

Granular Materials

Avalanche dynamics, jamming criticality, and self-organized criticality in granular sandpile and shear-cell models.
